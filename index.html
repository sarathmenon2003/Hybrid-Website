Electrical Services

Safe, code-compliant Low Voltage power distribution, switchgear assembly, and lighting infrastructure.

Turnkey DEWA and authority-compliant electrical execution—from load schedules, voltage-drop calculations, and shop drawings to panel termination, live testing, and final commissioning.

  • LV Switchgear (MDB / SMDB / DB): Installation, testing, commissioning & panel upgrading
  • Power Distribution: Main feeder cabling, sub-main circuits & heavy machinery wiring
  • Control & Automation Panels: Custom assembly, internal wiring & motor control centers
  • Lighting & Feeder Pillars: Interior, architectural façade, emergency & outdoor enclosures
